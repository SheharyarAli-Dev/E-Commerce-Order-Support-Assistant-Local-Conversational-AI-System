# ShopBot Store — Return, Refund & Shipping Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

---

## 1. Return Policy

### 1.1 Return Window
- You may return most items within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange.
- Items must be in their **original, unused condition** and in **original packaging** (all tags attached, all accessories included).

### 1.2 Non-Returnable Items
The following items **cannot be returned**:
- Digital products and downloadable software
- Perishable goods
- Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
- Undergarments and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Items that have been used, damaged by the customer, or are missing original packaging

### 1.3 Damaged or Defective Items
If your item arrives **damaged or defective**:
- Contact us within **7 days** of delivery with a photo of the damage.
- We will arrange a **free return** and issue a **full refund** or **replacement** at your preference.
- You do **not** need to pay return shipping for damaged or defective items.

### 1.4 How to Start a Return
1. Have your **Order ID** ready (format: ORD-XXXXX).
2. Tell ShopBot you'd like to return an item — we will log your return request.
3. A **pre-paid return label** will be emailed to your order email address within **1 business day**.
4. Drop the item at any carrier location shown on the label.
5. Refunds are processed after the item is received and inspected.

---

## 2. Refund Policy

### 2.1 Refund Timeline
- Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within **3–5 business days**.
- The refund is issued to the **original payment method** used at checkout.
- Depending on your bank, it may take an additional **2–5 business days** for the refund to appear on your statement.

### 2.2 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued in these cases:
- Item is returned used or in non-original condition (restocking fee of up to 20% may apply)
- Item is returned after the 30-day window but within 45 days (store credit only, no cash refund)

### 2.3 Original Shipping Cost
- Original shipping charges are **non-refundable**, unless the return is due to our error (wrong item sent, damaged item).

### 2.4 What We Cannot Do
- We **cannot** process refunds in cash or by check — all refunds go to the original payment method.
- We **cannot** issue refunds for orders older than **45 days** from delivery.
- ShopBot (the AI assistant) **cannot** directly initiate refunds — this requires a human support agent to confirm and process. ShopBot can log your request and escalate.

---

## 3. Exchange Policy

### 3.1 Exchanges
- Exchanges are allowed for **different sizes or colors of the same item** within **30 days** of delivery.
- Item must be unused and in original packaging.
- If the replacement variant is out of stock, we will offer a **full refund** instead.

### 3.2 How to Exchange
- Follow the same process as a return (Step 1.4 above).
- Specify in your return request that you want an **exchange** and your preferred variant.

---

## 4. Shipping Policy

### 4.1 Standard Shipping
- **Cost**: Free on orders over **$50**. Orders under $50 incur a flat **$5.99** shipping fee.
- **Estimated delivery**: 5–8 business days from the order date.
- **Carriers**: FedEx, UPS, USPS (carrier assigned based on destination and availability).

### 4.2 Expedited Shipping
- **Cost**: $12.99 flat rate.
- **Estimated delivery**: 2–3 business days.
- Available at checkout for all in-stock items.

### 4.3 Overnight Shipping
- **Cost**: $24.99 flat rate.
- **Estimated delivery**: Next business day (order must be placed before 1 PM EST).

### 4.4 International Shipping
- Currently, we **do not ship internationally**. Shipping is available within the **United States only**.

### 4.5 Shipping Restrictions
- P.O. Boxes: Accepted for USPS only. FedEx and UPS shipments require a physical address.
- Large or heavy items (e.g., IronGrip Dumbbells) may have an additional handling fee displayed at checkout.

### 4.6 Order Processing Time
- In-stock items are typically processed and dispatched within **1–2 business days** of order placement.
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.
- You will receive a **shipping confirmation email** with your tracking number once dispatched.

### 4.7 Tracking Your Order
- A tracking number is emailed when your order ships.
- You can also ask ShopBot your order status at any time using your **Order ID** (format: ORD-XXXXX).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My tracking hasn't updated in 3 days. What should I do?**
A: Tracking updates can sometimes lag by 24–48 hours during high-volume periods. If there's been no movement for more than 3 business days, contact us and we'll investigate with the carrier.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes are possible only if the order is still in "Processing" status. Once shipped, we cannot redirect the package. Contact support immediately if you need to change an address.

**Q: My order shows "Delivered" but I haven't received it.**
A: Check with neighbors and around your delivery location. If not found within 24 hours, contact us — we'll file a carrier claim and arrange a replacement or refund.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Orders can only be cancelled while in "Processing" status. Once shipped, cancellation is no longer possible — you would need to initiate a return after delivery. ShopBot can log a cancellation request for orders in Processing status, but cannot guarantee it will be actioned before shipping.
